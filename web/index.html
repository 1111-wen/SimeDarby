    About this app

    Sponge City Framework (MSMA / JPS): Models Green (urban parks, green roofs, rain gardens), Blue (wetlands, retention basins, widened rivers), and Gray (permeable pavements, cisterns, culverts) infrastructure. As water rises to Warning (≥75%) and Danger (≥85%), overtopping triggers dynamic evacuation rerouting away from flooded roads toward safety / relief centres (Masjid Bandar Bukit Raja).

    This app supports official warnings. Always follow instructions from Bomba and the authorities, and call 999 in an emergency.
