# Data sources and assumptions

SDP requires every data source, assumption and simulated dataset to be stated clearly.
Fill in the "Status" column as you go and copy this table into your submission.

## Data sources

| Data | Source | Used for | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Roads, buildings, rivers, lakes, bridges | OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), via OSMnx and OpenFreeMap tiles | Road network, 3D map | Public |
| Ground elevation | FABDEM (30 m, buildings/trees removed) or Copernicus GLO-30 | Flood model terrain | Public (state which one and its version) |
| Flood depths over time | HEC-RAS 2D (USACE), run by the team | Road risk and flood areas | Simulated by team |
| Rainfall scenarios | MSMA design storms (JPS) and/or JPS rainfall records | Scenario inputs (mm/h, duration) | Public; state which storms |
| Live rainfall (roadmap) | MET Malaysia (data.gov.my) / JPS Public Infobanjir | Choosing the matching scenario | Not yet connected |
| Relief centres | JKM disaster information / district office announcements | Destinations | Verify each one; record date |
| Past flood observations | News reports, resident survey, photos | Checking the model | Collected by team |
| Sample data in `web/data/sample` | Generated by `make_sample_data.py` | Testing and demo only | SYNTHETIC (invented) |

## Assumptions

| Assumption | Value | Why / note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drive mode: moderate risk | 10–30 cm of water | Low cars can stall; agree with authorities |
| Drive mode: high risk | 30 cm or more | Commonly cited guidance: about 30 cm of moving water can float many cars (e.g. US National Weather Service) |
| Walk mode: moderate risk | 5–15 cm of water | Slippery, hidden hazards such as open drains |
| Walk mode: high risk | 15 cm or more | Commonly cited guidance: about 15 cm of moving water can knock a person over (e.g. US National Weather Service) |
| Driving speed in floods | 20 km/h | Used only for the time estimate |
| Look-ahead window | 30 minutes | Roads that will become risky soon are treated as risky now |
| Walking speed | 4.5 km/h | Used only for the time estimate |
| Bridge risk | From approach roads | Deck height not modelled |
| Drain capacity | (fill in) | HEC-RAS 2D alone doesn't model pipes; state how drains are handled |
| Model resolution | (fill in, e.g. 30 m DEM, 10 m mesh) | Coarse elevation limits accuracy in flat areas |
|Gated communities | Internal roads treated as open	| Residents and emergency vehicles can pass the guardhouse; visitors may not |

## Known limits

- Predictions are only as good as the elevation data; flat areas like Klang need finer data.
- The model is checked against reported past floods, not calibrated with sensors yet.
- Live rainfall and sensor feeds are on the roadmap, not in the prototype.
