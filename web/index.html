    About this app

    Sponge township. Shows green (parks and green areas), blue (wetlands, ponds and lakes) and grey (drains and culverts) infrastructure. The storage meter estimates how full this system is; Warning (75% or more) and Danger (85% or more) are our own assumed levels. When storage is near full, routes shift away from flooding roads toward safety, such as Masjid Bandar Bukit Raja, which was used as a relief centre in past floods. Check official announcements for which centres are open.

    Safety. This app supports official warnings and never replaces them. Always follow Bomba and the authorities, and call 999 in an emergency.
